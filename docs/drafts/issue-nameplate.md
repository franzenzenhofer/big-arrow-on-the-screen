Title: Idea: a positioned arrow for nameplate attention

`nameplate attention` is close to what agents need when they want the human's hand. What it lacks is pointing at a specific spot. `bigarrow` (MIT, https://github.com/franzenzenhofer/big-arrow-on-the-screen) does that as a standalone CLI (click-through panel, curved arrow with a sign, start/stop or click to close). Would a `--at x,y` option for `attention`, or a mention of `bigarrow` for the pointing case, be welcome?
